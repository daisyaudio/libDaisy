daisy::DigitalControl Struct Reference

Detailed Description

Debouncer for non-GPIO switch inputs independent of their source. For getting Switch like behavior from inputs connected to shiftregisters, etc.

Maintains the state and allows checks for transitions, timing, etc.

Unlike AnalogControl, this does not take a pointer to an externally updated value. This is because the input to this may often be a callable (e.g. shiftreg.State(x), gpio.Read()) that may not be stateful.
